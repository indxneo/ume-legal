Ume

Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026

The short version: Ume keeps everything on your iPhone. There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the developer never receives your information.

Some optional features use Apple Intelligence. When they do, the text they need goes to Apple, as explained below.

What Ume keeps on your iPhone

  • The people you add: their names, how you know them, their dates (birthdays, anniversaries, days you remember someone, and anniversaries of a loss), your notes about them, the moments and gift ideas you save, who messages about them go to, and how you usually celebrate them.
  • History: messages you sent from Ume or marked as sent, things you marked as done, and any picture you made for a message.
  • “Your Voice”: up to 10 of your own messages, so drafts sound like you.
  • Your settings, such as your reminder time and appearance.

This information is stored only on your iPhone, inside Ume’s own storage, which its widgets and its Share extension also read. Ume has no servers. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, Ume’s data is included in that backup, under Apple’s terms. Deleting a person deletes what Ume keeps about them, and deleting the app deletes everything.

Contacts

Ume reads Contacts only if you choose to import people, and only the contacts you allow. It reads their names and dates, and remembers which contact each person came from. When you send a message, Ume reads that contact’s mobile number at that moment to fill in Messages. It never stores phone numbers and never uploads your contacts.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone with Apple’s notification system. Nothing is sent anywhere to schedule them. Because notifications can appear on a locked screen, they never name gift ideas, and a reminder about someone’s loss doesn’t mention the loss.

Message drafts (Apple Intelligence)

Drafts are optional, and you always edit and send messages yourself.

  • On your iPhone: with Apple Intelligence on your device, drafts can be written entirely on your iPhone. Nothing leaves it.
  • Better Drafts (on by default where available): drafts are written by Apple’s Private Cloud Compute. For each draft, Ume sends only what the draft needs: the first names of the person and of whoever gets the message, the occasion and how many years it marks, your relationship, your own words for who someone lost (for those dates), up to three moments you picked, and up to three of your past messages as examples of your style. Ume does not send your notes about the person, gift ideas, phone numbers or anyone else’s details. Apple states that Private Cloud Compute doesn’t store this information and that not even Apple can access it. You can turn Better Drafts off in Ume’s Settings to keep every draft on your iPhone.
  • Without Apple Intelligence, Ume uses its own written starters, and nothing is sent.

Pictures (Image Playground)

When writing a message, you can make a picture with Apple’s Image Playground. The description you type is given to Image Playground, and Apple makes the picture. Apple’s privacy policy covers that. Ume receives the finished picture and keeps it on your iPhone with the message. Pictures are labeled as made with Image Playground. Ume never sends names or notes to Image Playground, only what you type.

Sending messages

Messages go through Apple’s Messages app, and you tap Send yourself. Ume never sends anything on its own and never sees what Messages does with your message.

Siri, Spotlight, widgets and sharing

  • The names of the people you add are indexed on your iPhone, so you can find them in Spotlight and use them with Siri and Shortcuts. Moments and notes are never indexed.
  • Widgets show names, occasions and countdowns, never moments or gift ideas. Anyone who can see your Home Screen or Lock Screen can see what a widget shows.
  • What you share to Ume from other apps is saved on your iPhone.

Backups you export

If you export a backup from Settings, Ume creates a file with your people, dates, notes, moments, history and Your Voice (pictures aren’t included). You choose where it goes, so treat it as private.

What the developer receives

Nothing. Ume contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and has no account or server. If you allow your iPhone to share analytics with app developers, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with the developer. You control that in your iPhone’s Settings, under Privacy & Security.

Children

Ume isn’t directed at children under 13, and it doesn’t collect information from anyone.
